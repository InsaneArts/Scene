# Scene 0.1.0

One theme for your whole Mac: the wallpaper, Light/Dark, the accent color, Ghostty, iTerm2, VS Code, Cursor, and Neovim change together, and Scene can put your original setup back.

- 75 themes, with three backgrounds for every look.
- The switcher over every screen, the menu bar panel, and Undo.
- Scene checks for updates. Turn it off in Settings, under Updates.
