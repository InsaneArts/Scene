# Art styles

Styles and concepts for art-first themes (Art-first in [WALLPAPERS.md](WALLPAPERS.md)), from research in September 2026. A theme remixes one style with a computing, hacking, or science subject, and its name is a developer's pun on the subject, never the artist.

## Rules

- **Describe the technique.** Medium, support, marks, composition, and light: "oil on canvas, thick impasto in short directional strokes, a swirling cobalt sky, chrome-yellow halos". A painter's name pulls the picture toward that painter's most famous composition; a movement name ("Edo-period woodblock print") helps without that pull.
- **Public domain only.** Imitate artists who died before 1955 (in the public domain in the EU) and anonymous traditions. In the US, works published in 1931 or later can still be protected, so late Kandinsky, Mondrian, Klee, Munch, Malevich, and Mucha are safer described than named. OpenAI refuses the styles of living artists.
- **Estates.** Escher, Picasso, Dalí, Magritte, Hopper, and Hergé are still protected, and several estates enforce their rights: describe the look ("impossible architecture", "ligne claire") and never keep a known work's composition with a new subject in it.
- **Culture.** No Aboriginal dot painting. No saints, deities, or other sacred figures: keep the technique (mosaic, stained glass, illumination) and paint a secular subject. No calligraphy of scripture, no rising-sun rays in Japanese prints, no hammer and sickle, stars, or slogans in Constructivism, and no war imagery with Futurism.
- **Exclusions to add.** Seals and inscriptions (ink, woodblock), cartouches and plate numbers (prints, natural-history plates), monograms and dates (engravings), captions, and painted frames. Old devices drift toward real products: ask for generic, unbranded hardware.
- **Darkness.** Many originals are mid-tone (Klimt, Turner, shan shui). For a dark look, name the dark ground or the night.

## What the community likes

From r/unixporn's top posts, 2024 to September 2026, and the wallpaper collections ricers use:

- Public-domain paintings appear in only 1 to 3% of top rices, but they score well, often as a "no anime" statement.
- The best-scoring art is monochrome ink on paper: engraving, etching, woodcut. A Dürer engraving scored 805 ("a digital scholar's desk"), and supply is thin. Next come European oil painting from 1600 to 1900 (a vanitas scored 1,333), pixel art, woodblock prints other than the Great Wave, painterly skies (Turner, Constable, Church), Chinese ink wash, Morris-style pattern, and dither or halftone.
- Almost nobody has used Art Nouveau, Klimt, Mucha, Haeckel plates, or fine art remixed with computing: a sign of open ground, not proof of demand.
- Overused: the Great Wave, Starry Night remixes, the Creation of Adam, neon cyberpunk, vaporwave statues, lo-fi, and anime.
- AI wallpapers are tolerated, not banned. Disclosure and craft win, and glossy futurism reads as slop. Scene credits every generated wallpaper as "Generated for Scene with GPT Image".

## Prompting

- Order the subject as scene, subject, details, then exclusions, and name the calm area as a thing: "a wide band of empty mist", "blank vellum", "plain dark ground".
- Say where the detail goes: "at the lower left", "along the bottom and up the right edge".
- Wallpapers 2 and 3 take wallpaper 1 as `--reference` and need their own subject and framing: a wide scene, a close-up, an object.
- Change one thing at a time when a picture fails, and keep the exclusions in every attempt. A `moderation_blocked` error means the prompt must change, not the retry count.
